# Content Rules (mandatory)

1. **Restrained horror.** Imply, don't show: silhouettes, shadows, reactions, sound. **No gore**: no exposed organs, dismemberment, close-ups of wounds, torture, or lingering death. Use dark stains/torn cloth sparingly. Violence against zombies shown as impact, cut-away, or off-screen.
2. **Children:** never depict children being harmed or turned in an on-screen way; keep child characters safe/offscreen in danger; no child zombies unless the script is clearly a fictional stylised short and kept non-graphic (default: avoid).
3. **Real-world respect:** don't use real disasters, epidemics (COVID etc.), massacres or real tragedies as comedic fodder; if referencing, keep factual and sombre. Don't depict any real ethnic/religious/national group as the "infected" or as villains. Avoid mocking disability/mental illness; avoid using real people's likeness as zombies.
4. **Sacred places & symbols:** show with respect; never as monster dens or desecrated for shock.
5. **Survival information:** only widely accepted, low-risk guidance (water, shelter, hygiene, signalling, basic first aid). **No instructions** for making weapons, explosives, traps that can harm people, drugs, or dangerous chemical reactions. No medical dosing. Add optional disclaimer: "This is a work of fiction. In a real emergency, follow guidance from local authorities and qualified experts."
6. **Weapons:** minimal, non-glorified, finite, noisy; avoid detailed handling/modification tutorials.
7. **Panic/anxiety:** avoid content that could encourage real panic (fake news bulletins must be clearly styled as fiction – include "FICTION" tag on any news-style overlay).
8. **Copyright:** no existing franchise characters, logos, or iconic designs; original designs only.
9. **Audio safety:** avoid sudden extreme loudness; add content warning for intense scenes if script is dark.
10. **Platform-friendly:** keep thumbnails/frames non-graphic (no blood close-ups).
