# Cast, Creatures & Props (build system)

## Human construction (locked)
Flat 2D jointed cut-out; thin dark outline; soft shadow; ≈ 7 heads tall adults, 5 for teens/children; slim; large almond eyes; small nose/mouth; **same face construction for everyone**; identity via skin tone, hair, clothing, gear, posture. Use a **diverse skin palette** (`#5b3a29 · #8d5a3b · #b97a4b · #d9a066 · #e8b57a · #f1c9a0 · #f6dcc0`). Expressions through brows, eyelids, mouth; **fear/exhaustion shown by posture + eye bags + sweat drops + shaky outline offset**.

Prompt formula:
`[role/name], flat 2D paper-cutout animation style, [skin tone], [hair], [clothing, worn/dirty detail], [gear], [expression/posture], thin dark outline, soft drop shadow, full body, plain background`

## Survivor archetypes (mix; give each a flaw + a need)
| Archetype | Look | Gear | Flaw → story use |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Planner (teacher/engineer) | glasses, backpack, notebook | map, pen, compass | over-cautious → delays |
| The Medic (nurse/pharmacist) | scrubs under jacket, tired eyes | first-aid kit | refuses to leave wounded |
| The Mechanic/Driver | overalls, cap, grease marks | wrench, keys, jerrycan | reckless confidence |
| The Teen Scavenger | hoodie/school bag, sneakers | slingshot/torch, snacks | impulsive, brave |
| The Ex-Soldier/Guard (restrained) | cargo pants, vest, scars | crowbar/bat, radio | trust issues |
| The Parent | worn clothes, protective stance | child's toy, water bottle | emotional decisions |
| The Elder | stick/spectacles, shawl/jacket | herbal kit, wisdom | physical limits |
| The Stranger | hood, face partly hidden | unknown bag | hidden motive (twist tool) |
Give **names, one signature item, one habit** (e.g., always counts supplies). Audience attachment = retention.

## Zombie construction ("infected")
- Same cut-out puppet style, **sickly desaturated skin** (ash grey, olive, pale blue-grey), **hollow dark eye sockets with pale/yellow-white eyes**, slack jaw, torn everyday clothes (office shirt, school uniform, saree/kurta, uniforms) – they were people; vary age/gender/culture.
- **Restraint:** no exposed organs, no gore. Use **dark stains, dirt, torn cloth, bandages, scratched skin lines** only.
- Motion identity per type; sound identity per type.

| Type | Silhouette | Behaviour (default; define in rulebook) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walker | normal build, hunched | slow, drawn to noise | baseline threat, crowds |
| Runner | lean, forward lean | fast, short stamina | chase beats |
| Crawler | torso only/dragging | silent-ish, low | ankle-level scares |
| Screamer | open-mouth, glowing throat | shriek attracts horde | noise-risk set-pieces |
| Bloater/Heavy | wide body | slow, tanky, blocks doors | obstacle / boss beat |
| Stalker | ragged, still | stands motionless until you move | suspense |
| Alpha (optional) | taller, calm eyes | commands/herds | series arc |
**Horde** = many identical silhouettes with colour jitter + 3–4 hero zombies in front. Show scale via **silhouette wall in fog** rather than detail.

## Props library (consistent designs)
Torch (cone beam) · candle/lantern · crowbar · cricket bat · kitchen knife (not emphasised) · slingshot · rope · duct tape · water bottles/jerrycans · canned food · rice/atta bags · first-aid kit · bandage rolls · hand-crank radio · walkie-talkie · map & marker · padlock/chain · plywood barricade · nails/hammer · bicycle · bike · auto-rickshaw · truck/bus · generator · solar lamp · phone with cracked screen · power bank · diary · keys · caution tape · noise-makers (cans on string, alarm clocks) · fire barrel.
Firearms: if the script has them, show them **rarely, finite ammo, loud consequence** (noise ring on screen). No loving gun close-ups.

## Vehicles & structures
Hatchback, jeep, bus, truck, auto-rickshaw, train coach, ambulance (abandoned), boat. Buildings: apartment block, school, mall, hospital, police chowki, godown, temple/mosque/church courtyard, farmhouse, water tower, rooftop.

## Name ribbons
Amber = survivors · grey = places · rust/red = zombie types · teal = allies/radio voices · black/red = threats/unknown.
